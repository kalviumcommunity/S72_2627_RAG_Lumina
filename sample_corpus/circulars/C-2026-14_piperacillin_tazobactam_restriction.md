## 1 Background

Piperacillin-tazobactam use across DHN rose by 38% in the 12 months to August 2026, and resistance among hospital-acquired Gram-negative isolates increased over the same period. The Antimicrobial Stewardship Committee has therefore reclassified piperacillin-tazobactam as a restricted antimicrobial.

## 2 Revised unrestricted list

In partial modification of DG-01 §3.1 (Restricted Antimicrobials Policy, version 4), the list of unrestricted antimicrobials is replaced by the table below. Piperacillin-tazobactam is no longer unrestricted.

| Agent | Route | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Amoxicillin-clavulanate | IV or oral | First line for community-acquired intra-abdominal infection |
| Ceftriaxone | IV | First line for community-acquired pneumonia requiring admission |
| Azithromycin | IV or oral | Atypical pneumonia cover |
| Metronidazole | IV or oral | Anaerobic cover |
| Doxycycline | Oral | Atypical cover and skin infections |

## 3 Revised restricted list

DG-01 §3.2 is amended so that piperacillin-tazobactam is added to the restricted antimicrobials. Restricted agents need AMS pharmacist or ID consultant approval within 24 hours of the first dose; the first dose must not be delayed while approval is sought.

| Agent | Route | Typical indication |
|---|---|---|
| Piperacillin-tazobactam | IV | Hospital-acquired infection and neutropenic sepsis |
| Meropenem | IV | Suspected ESBL-producing organisms or failure of first-line therapy |
| Vancomycin | IV | MRSA infection or severe beta-lactam allergy |
| Linezolid | IV or oral | VRE or MRSA when vancomycin is unsuitable |
| Teicoplanin | IV | MRSA infection when vancomycin is unsuitable |
| Cefepime | IV | Pseudomonas cover when carbapenems are to be spared |
