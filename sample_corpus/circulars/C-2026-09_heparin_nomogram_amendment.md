## 1 Background

A quality review of DHN intensive care units in the second quarter of 2026 found that, under the existing hold-and-decrease step, 31% of aPTT results above 100 seconds were followed by a second result above 100 seconds. The Drugs and Therapeutics Committee has therefore approved a revised action for supra-therapeutic aPTT results.

## 2 Amendment to the aPTT nomogram

In partial modification of P-ICU-07 §4.2 (Heparin Infusion Protocol, version 3), the aPTT adjustment nomogram is replaced by the table below with effect from 1 September 2026. All other sections of P-ICU-07 remain in force. The therapeutic target remains an aPTT of 60–85 seconds.

| aPTT (seconds) | Bolus | Infusion action | Next aPTT |
|---|---|---|---|
| Below 40 | 80 units/kg | Increase by 4 units/kg/h | 6 hours |
| 40–59 | 40 units/kg | Increase by 2 units/kg/h | 6 hours |
| 60–85 | None | No change | Next morning |
| 86–100 | None | Decrease by 2 units/kg/h | 6 hours |
| Above 100 | None | Stop the infusion for 1 hour, then restart at a rate reduced by 3 units/kg/h and inform the ICU pharmacist | 6 hours after restart |

## 3 Action required

- ICU nurses in charge: brief all nursing staff on the new step for aPTT results above 100 seconds before 7 September 2026.
- Pharmacy: update the smart-pump drug library and the electronic prescribing order set to the revised nomogram.
- Remove printed copies of the old nomogram (P-ICU-07 version 3, section 4.2) from bedside folders by 7 September 2026.
