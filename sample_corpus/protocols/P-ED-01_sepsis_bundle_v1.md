## 1 Purpose

This pathway describes the three-hour sepsis bundle used in DHN emergency departments for adults with suspected sepsis.

## 5 The three-hour bundle

### 5.1 Lactate

Measure a lactate within 3 hours of arrival.

### 5.3 Antibiotics

Give intravenous antibiotics within 3 hours of arrival in the emergency department for every patient with suspected sepsis.

### 5.4 Fluids

Give 20 mL/kg of intravenous crystalloid for hypotension within 3 hours of arrival.
