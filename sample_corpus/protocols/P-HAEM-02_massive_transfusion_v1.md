## 1 Purpose

This protocol describes how to activate, run and stand down the massive transfusion protocol (MTP) for adults with life-threatening haemorrhage, so that blood components arrive quickly and in a balanced ratio.

## 2 Activation criteria

Activate the MTP for an adult with major haemorrhage when any of the following applies: blood loss estimated at more than 150 mL/min; a need for 4 or more units of red cells within 1 hour; or a shock index (heart rate divided by systolic blood pressure) above 1 with ongoing bleeding. The team leader decides on activation.

## 3 How to activate

- Call the blood bank emergency line on extension 2210 and say "Massive transfusion protocol activation", giving the patient's location and the name of the team leader.
- Send a group and screen sample and a coagulation screen, including fibrinogen, immediately.
- Nominate one staff member as the transfusion runner for the whole episode.

## 4 MTP packs

### 4.1 Pack contents

The blood bank issues packs in the sequence below until the MTP is stood down.

| Pack | Red cells | FFP | Platelets | Cryoprecipitate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pack 1 | 4 units | 4 units | None | None |
| Pack 2 | 4 units | 4 units | 1 adult dose | 10 units if fibrinogen is below 1.5 g/L |
| Pack 3 onwards | Alternate Pack 1 and Pack 2 | As per pack | As per pack | Guided by fibrinogen |

### 4.2 Issue times

The blood bank aims to issue Pack 1 within 15 minutes of activation. Two units of group O red cells are available immediately from the emergency department blood fridge while Pack 1 is prepared.

## 5 Adjuncts and targets

- Tranexamic acid: 1 g IV over 10 minutes within 3 hours of injury, followed by 1 g IV over 8 hours.
- Keep the ionised calcium above 1.1 mmol/L; give 10 mL of calcium chloride 10% IV if it falls below this level.
- Targets during resuscitation: fibrinogen above 1.5 g/L, platelets above 75 x 10^9/L, INR below 1.5 and temperature above 36 °C.

## 6 Standing down

The team leader stands down the MTP by calling extension 2210 once the bleeding is controlled. Return all unused components to the blood bank within 30 minutes so that they can be reissued.
