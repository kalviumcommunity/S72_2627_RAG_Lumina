## 1 Purpose

This protocol describes the use of intravenous unfractionated heparin infusions for adult patients in DHN intensive care units, including the starting dose, the aPTT adjustment nomogram and the monitoring schedule.

## 2 Scope

Adult patients (16 years and older) receiving therapeutic intravenous unfractionated heparin in a DHN intensive care unit. Paediatric patients and circuit anticoagulation are excluded.

## 4 Dosing

### 4.1 Initial bolus and infusion

- Loading bolus: 70 units/kg IV (maximum 8,000 units).
- Starting infusion rate: 15 units/kg/h (maximum starting rate 1,500 units/h).
- Standard concentration: 25,000 units in 250 mL glucose 5% (100 units/mL).

### 4.2 aPTT adjustment nomogram

Adjust the infusion according to the aPTT. The therapeutic target in this version is an aPTT of 50–70 seconds.

| aPTT (seconds) | Bolus | Infusion action | Next aPTT |
|---|---|---|---|
| Below 35 | 70 units/kg | Increase by 3 units/kg/h | 8 hours |
| 35–49 | 35 units/kg | Increase by 2 units/kg/h | 8 hours |
| 50–70 | None | No change | Next morning |
| 71–90 | None | Decrease by 1 unit/kg/h | 8 hours |
| Above 90 | None | Hold the infusion for 30 minutes, then decrease by 2 units/kg/h | 8 hours |

### 4.3 Monitoring

- Repeat the aPTT 8 hours after starting the infusion and 8 hours after every rate change.
- Check the platelet count every 48 hours.
