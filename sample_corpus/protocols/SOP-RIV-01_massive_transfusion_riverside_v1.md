## 1 Purpose

DHN Riverside has no on-site blood bank. This standard operating procedure sets out the local arrangements that apply at Riverside in addition to P-HAEM-02 (Massive Transfusion Protocol). Where this SOP differs from P-HAEM-02, this SOP applies at Riverside.

## 2 Activation at Riverside

- Call the Riverside transfusion coordinator on extension 4410 (staffed 24 hours) to activate the massive transfusion protocol at Riverside. Do not call extension 2210, which serves the Central blood bank only.
- The transfusion coordinator requests the MTP packs from the Central blood bank and books the emergency courier.
- The team leader nominates a transfusion runner, as in P-HAEM-02.

## 3 Emergency stock at Riverside

Riverside holds 4 units of group O negative red cells and 2 units of pre-thawed group AB plasma in the emergency department satellite fridge. Use this emergency stock first, and record every unit used on the MTP log sheet so that the coordinator can replace it.

## 4 Transport time

Expect Pack 1 to arrive at Riverside about 35 minutes after activation. Continue with the emergency stock until the courier arrives, and inform the coordinator on extension 4410 if the patient is being transferred to Central.
